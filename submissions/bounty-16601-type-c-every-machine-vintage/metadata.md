## Description

RustChain’s Proof of Antiquity is designed around verified physical hardware and longevity rather than raw speed alone. In the project’s current README, a PowerPC G4 from 2003 is listed at a 2.5× multiplier while modern x86_64 is listed at 0.8×. The same documentation illustrates how one example machine’s multiplier can grow as it ages.

This short explains the idea without converting multipliers into dollar earnings or making investment claims.
