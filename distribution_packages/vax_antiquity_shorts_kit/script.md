# Narration Script

**Target duration:** 40–50 seconds  
**Word count:** 102

> Most blockchains reward faster hardware. RustChain does something different.
>
> In its current RIP-200 reward table, the DEC VAX architecture has a **3.5x base antiquity multiplier**.
>
> That does **not** mean a VAX is guaranteed 3.5 times more RTC. The node time-ages antiquity weights, applies attestation and eligibility checks, and then distributes the epoch’s fixed reward by weight.
>
> Block production is separate: RIP-200 describes deterministic round-robin selection — **one CPU, one vote** — instead of a fastest-hash lottery.
>
> So old hardware can carry more reward weight without buying extra block-production turns.
>
> The number is public in the node code: `"vax": 3.5`.
>
> In RustChain, vintage silicon is part of the economics, not just a museum piece.

## Accuracy note

Say “base antiquity multiplier” or “base weight,” not “guaranteed earnings.” Reward share depends on the live eligibility and weighting path.
