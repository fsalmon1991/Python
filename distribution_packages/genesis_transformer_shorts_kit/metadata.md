## Short description
A source-grounded look at Elyan Labs’ `legend-of-elya-genesis` project: an integer-only ternary transformer running on a Sega Genesis Model 1. The short separates the real-hardware ~1.97 tok/s observation from the project’s headless MAME exact-cycle benchmark, which reports a 1.674× optimization from 220,579,814 to 131,766,162 cycles.
